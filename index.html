AURA

Academic University Registration Architecture

A multi-tenant SaaS platform for university registration, scheduling and grading, built by a four-person team at Zewail City and nominated for the Medal of Excellence in Entrepreneurship. It now runs as an active startup venture, accepted into a pre-incubation programme after a competition interview and judging panel.

The platform runs on a self-hosted server in Egypt and bursts into Azure only when the on-premises node is saturated — enterprise availability at roughly the cost of a coffee per month. My work sat in the application and security layer.

  • Registration logic that respects the calendar

    Semester and registration-period scoping across dashboards, schedules and enrolment queries — single active period enforcement, per-semester section preservation, and correct behaviour for past and completed terms.

  • Professor and dean workflows

    Built the professor dashboard and the grade-change request workflow with dean approval, plus admin panel operations for periods, semesters and majors, scoped by university_id for multi-tenant isolation.

  • Security remediation

    Worked through the critical and high findings from the project's code scanning, alongside the formal OWASP/PTES penetration test the team ran — 13 findings remediated before submission.

EcoForge GEMP

Green Energy Monitoring Platform

A budget allocator for public-building retrofits, built for RoboDam 2026. I designed and implemented the whole system as the sole engineer on a two-person team — domain model, telemetry ingestion, optimisation engine, API and front end — and shipped it as a containerised stack.

  • Tamper-evident telemetry

    Every meter reading is HMAC-signed and hash-chained on arrival. Chain heads are anchored outside the database volume, so an altered or truncated record is detected, and a verification endpoint reports where a chain first breaks.

  • Access control that records refusals

    RBAC with an audit log covering denied actions as well as permitted ones, and no default credentials — the deployment ships with a one-time bootstrap flow.

  • A CI pipeline that actually blocks

    Bandit, Semgrep, pip-audit, Gitleaks, Hadolint and Trivy across filesystem, config, IaC and image. Container builds are signed, and every security exception carries an expiry date that fails the build once it lapses.

05 — Profile

A bit more

I graduated from Zewail City in July 2026 with a B.Sc. in Computational Science and Artificial Intelligence, majoring in Information Technology with a concentration in Networks, Security and Governance. My work sits where cloud architecture meets security engineering: designing the infrastructure, then making it defensible.

Two things shape how I build. First, a system should be able to demonstrate its own state — failing over on its own, or walking a chain of records back and saying exactly where trust ends. Second, security belongs in the pipeline rather than in a review at the end; a gate that blocks on any finding is a gate people actually design around.

AURA is the project I care most about. It started as a graduation requirement and turned into something four of us are still building — a registration platform a university in a developing region can actually afford to run. Watching it hold five thousand concurrent users on a server sitting in Egypt, for a few dollars a month, is the most satisfying result I have been part of.

Before that: a summer inside a regulated bank's security team, a year in a university IT department building and hardening campus network infrastructure, and a semester teaching the network installation lab I had taken the year before.
